ON A PHONE
The landing artifacts form a living 3D orbit. Swipe to move through them and tap the focused object to enter its chapter.

QUICKEST WAY TO PUBLISH
Upload the CONTENTS of ready-to-publish to your GitHub repository.
Then Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > your branch > / (root).
Keep index.html, assets, and artifacts together.

TO EDIT THE WEBSITE
Use the source folder. See README.md for local setup and automatic GitHub publishing.
